      About

      btw, that's me

       

      I went from intern to project leader in 21 months, and honestly I think it's because I treat the interface as the product, not the paint job. Whether it's an AI platform helping doctors make clinical calls or a VS Code extension that packs commits into QA evidence, the job is the same: find the part of the workflow everyone quietly hates, and make it obvious.
